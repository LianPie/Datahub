Hassle-Free Organization

No technical knowledge required. With Datahub, you can create folders, upload files, and organize everything just like you would on your own personal computer. The interface is clean, familiar, and intuitive — so you spend less time figuring things out and more time getting things done. Whether you're managing work documents, study notes, or personal files, everything stays exactly where you expect it to be.

Security & Privacy First

Your important files deserve to stay safe. Datahub provides secure login and stores everything locally on your own environment — not on unknown third-party clouds. This means you don't have to worry about data leaks, hidden access, or unexpected privacy issues. What belongs to you stays with you. We believe good file management shouldn't come at the cost of your privacy.

Smart File Management

Accidental deletion? Not a problem. When you delete a file, it moves to the trash bin first — not permanently erased. You can restore it anytime with one click. On top of that, Datahub shows you exactly how much storage space you're using and lets you filter your files by creation date. It's a small but powerful way to keep your digital life under control, without surprises.
